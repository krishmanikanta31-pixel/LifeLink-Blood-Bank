Identity is checked against a government ID, and eligibility follows the national donor-selection rules.

Eligibility rules — NBTC Guidelines for Blood Donor Selection, 2017 (Govt. of India)
  • Age 18–65 years. A first-time donor must not be above 60.
  • Gap between whole-blood donations: at least 90 days for men and 120 days for women. Donors who identify as Other follow the longer 120-day gap.
  • One valid government ID per donor; the same ID cannot be registered twice.

ID check in this prototype: format rules for every ID type, plus the official Verhoeff checksum for Aadhaar. Only the last 4 characters are stored. Live verification with UIDAI / DigiLocker needs government API access and is listed as a future enhancement.
