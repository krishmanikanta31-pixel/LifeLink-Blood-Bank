Who can donate — NBTC Guidelines for Blood Donor Selection, 2017 (Govt. of India)
  • Age 18–65 years. A first-time donor must not be above 60.
  • Body weight at least 45 kg.
  • Gap between whole-blood donations: at least 90 days for men and 120 days for women. Donors who identify as Other follow the longer 120-day gap.
  • Haemoglobin must be at least 12.5 g/dL — this is tested at the blood bank before donation.
